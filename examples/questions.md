# Example research questions

Use these to demo baseline vs web-discovery upgrades:

1) **Framework choice**
- “Should I build my next AI agent with LangChain, LlamaIndex, or LangGraph in 2025? What are the tradeoffs?”

2) **Trend check**
- “What’s the current sentiment around Framework X? Any breaking changes, drama, or ‘gotchas’ people complain about?”

3) **Vendor evaluation**
- “Is Vendor A reliable for production? Look for outages, billing complaints, hidden limits, and security concerns.”

4) **Pricing reality**
- “What does it *actually* cost to run this at 100k queries/day? Find real user reports, not just marketing pages.”

Tip: the best prompts combine **what to decide** + **what to watch out for**.
